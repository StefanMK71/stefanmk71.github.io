Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29/04/2025

Last Updated: 15/05/2026

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

We securely collect your email and password when you sign up.

Location Data

We use your location to help you find and view nearby routes. Minimum location permissions needed are while the app is in use.

To be able to use the 'My Drives' feature, you will need to grant 'always' location permissions to allow Drively to track your drives reliably via GPS.

Your location data is not stored in any sense.

User Content

You can create routes, rate routes, and upload photos. This content is stored in Google Firebase.

Analytics & Crash Reports

We collect anonymous usage data and crash logs using Firebase Crashlytics to improve stability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display relevant routes near you.
  • To allow you to create, save, and rate routes.
  • To improve and maintain app performance.
  • To troubleshoot issues.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. Limited data may be shared with Firebase or when required by law.

4. Your Rights and Choices

You may delete your account at any time, which removes your data. Location sharing is optional but affects functionality.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security measures.

6. Children’s Privacy

Drively is not intended for people under 17. We do not collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. You will be notified of major changes via the app.

8. Drively Club

Drively offers optional in-app subscriptions (“Drively Club”) processed securely through Apple’s App Store. We do not collect or store payment card information. Subscription status may be used within the app to unlock premium features such as CarPlay support, navigation features, and ad-free browsing.
